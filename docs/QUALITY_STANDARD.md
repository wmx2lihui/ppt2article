# Scientific Paper Quality Standard

## Target

The generated manuscript should meet the standards of high-quality international journals.

## Scientific Requirements

- Clear research gap
- Explicit contribution
- Strong theoretical foundation
- Reproducible methodology
- Sufficient validation

## Writing Requirements

- Natural academic English
- Avoid generic AI expressions
- Strong logical transitions
- Consistent terminology

## Figure Requirements

- Vector PDF preferred
- Journal-compatible typography
- Clear captions
- Every figure supports the story

## Review Requirements

Before submission, simulate:

- Expert reviewer
- Methodology reviewer
- Editor decision process
