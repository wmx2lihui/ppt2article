# v3.3 Figure Intelligence

## Goal

Build an AI system that plans scientific figures according to the logic of a journal paper, rather than merely generating images.

## Pipeline

Research claim

-> Required evidence

-> Figure role

-> Visualization design

-> Caption generation

-> Manuscript integration

## Figure Types

- Conceptual framework figures
- Algorithm workflow figures
- Experimental comparison figures
- Mechanism illustrations
- Graphical abstracts

## Principle

Every figure should answer one scientific question and support one major claim.
